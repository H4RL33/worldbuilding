The [[Democratic Republic of Earth]] is a governing body below the [[ISCIA]] encompassing only [[Humanity]]-aligned planets and settlements.

It was founded out of intense xenophobia by [[Alexander White]], as the [[ISCIA]] became more and more inclusive of [[Extraterrestrial Intelligent Species]] which diluted the concentration of explicitly human political identity within its government.

Touted by critics as a government of unjust hatred and xenophobia, the [[Democratic Republic of Earth|DRE]] insists that it's founder's hatred has been rooted out of all of it's policies and constitution, though it remains strongly opposed to non-human species in it's governments.

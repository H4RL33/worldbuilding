The Human Parasite Prevention Order, [[HPPO]] is a terrorist group under the ideology that [[Humanity]] is naturally a [[Parasite]] and will only bring death, destruction, and suffering to any planet it sets foot on.
## Member Demographics
Surprisingly, the [[HPPO]] is comprised of a significant portion of [[Humanity]], as well as [[alien_1]], [[alien_2]] and [[alien_3]].

The [[HPPO]] is believed to be around 200-members strong.

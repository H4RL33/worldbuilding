The [[ICRCM|ISCIA Census Reconciliation and Concluding Meeting]] is a large gathering that occurs every four years a couple months after the [[ISC Census]] to allow each independent committee to prepare their findings.

The goal of the [[ICRCM]] is to compile the [[ISC Census#Census Concluding Statement of Facts|Census Concluding Statement of Facts]] which will be published to all committees under the [[ISCIA]] to allow them to make well-informed plans over the next four years according to the state of the population under the [[ISCIA]].

Typically, it takes several weeks to compile and finalise the statement of facts, with the [[ICRCM]] technically ongoing throughout that whole time, though of course attendees are free to leave and return respective of usual work hours like normal.

Furthermore, the [[ICRCM]] is repurposed for the annual smaller-scale optional census, which also compiles its own much smaller statement of facts. Due to the scale of this census, and the fewer responses, this [[ICRCM]] only typically takes a few days to a week as most committees come fully prepared.

The [[ISC Score]] system was founded to quantify a citizen's contributions to the betterment of society under the [[ISCIA]] as a whole.

Such a system was desired to determine how to prioritise government resources, such as humanitarian or financial aid.

Naturally, the [[ISC Score]] has received incredibly opposition, however the [[ISCIA]] remains adamant that it overall improves their efficiency and enables a better life for later generations.

The [[Present Day]] of the [[Immediate Stellar Cluster]] is chronologically equivalent to 1984 in the real world. The discovery of [[Isolation]], however, profoundly altered the direction of [[Humanity|human]] technological development. The Local Cluster consequently possesses capabilities far beyond those of the real-world present day, but only within a comparatively narrow group of disciplines.

Socially, discrimination and gendered injustice are less severe than they once were in earlier drafts. Progress was made in civil rights and women's rights, but institutions remain uneven and still fall short of contemporary standards.

## Technology

Technological development throughout the [[Immediate Stellar Cluster]] has been profoundly asymmetrical. Generations of concentrated investment accelerated [[Isolation]] and the fields upon which it depended, particularly energy generation, computation, precision engineering, gravitational science, and material fabrication. Other disciplines received substantially less attention and often remain comparable to, or less developed than, their real-world equivalents of the mid-twentieth century.

The Local Cluster has nearly trivialised interstellar transportation and possesses an exceptional capacity to model orbital systems, gravitational interactions, and [[Isolation#Isolation Drive|Isolation trajectories]]. Its governments and institutions can administer territories across multiple star systems, although this capacity depends as much upon communications and bureaucracy as upon transportation itself.

Artificial intelligence, as understood in the real-world present day, is virtually nonexistent. The most advanced computational systems are enormous numerical and statistical engines used to calculate interstellar routes, model hundreds of gravitational influences, identify potential obstructions, and regulate unstable Isolation fields. These machines can optimise solutions within carefully defined parameters, but they cannot reason generally, interpret unfamiliar situations, or independently acquire broad competencies.

Medical science is also still uneven. Many advanced surgical procedures available in the real world are unknown, prohibitively dangerous, or possible only within a handful of specialist institutions. Virology, immunology, tissue cultivation, and epidemiology remain particularly weak. [[Polio]], and a number of other vaccine-preventable diseases, have mostly been eradicated in larger settlements, though this succeeded later than in the real-world timeline.

Biology and food science have undergone similarly uneven development. Conventional selective breeding is still widespread, and molecular genetics is beginning to appear in better-funded institutions, but modern reproductive technologies and full genomic optimisation remain limited. Agricultural development therefore relies primarily upon land, labour, fertiliser, mechanisation, controlled environments, and recurrent selective-breeding cycles.

Food itself is rarely scarce across the Local Cluster, but its production is inefficient and extraordinarily extensive. Staple crops are cultivated on an immense scale, while exotic fruits, animal products, and foods requiring specialised climates remain considerably more expensive. Livestock grow more slowly and convert feed into edible mass less efficiently than modern real-world breeds. Meat is consequently an occasional luxury for much of the population and a regular component of the diet principally among wealthier households.

Military technology is less advanced than the Local Cluster's spacecraft and industrial infrastructure might suggest. Firearms are generally robust but heavy, mechanically simple, and lacking the lightweight materials, compact optics, electronic sights, and guided ammunition common in the real-world present day. Automatic weapons exist, but infantry models are cumbersome, while heavier and more reliable examples are commonly mounted aboard [[Spacecraft]], ground vehicles, and defensive installations.

Conventional explosives and precision-guided weapons are also comparatively underdeveloped. Although the Local Cluster possesses extensive knowledge of nuclear reactors and fissile materials, most nuclear research historically concerned energy production for early [[Isolation#Isolation Drive|Isolation drives]]. Thermonuclear weapon development received little comparable investment and later became subject to severe political restrictions. The largest [[Nuke|nuclear weapon]] yet detonated yielded approximately 445 kilotons of TNT.

Material science, by contrast, received enormous investment during the [[Sol Boom]]. The construction of [[Space Stations]], [[Spacecraft]], orbital industries, and [[Extraplanetary Cities]] demanded exceptionally strong alloys, ceramics, composites, radiation-resistant materials, and highly precise industrial fabrication.

Combined with computer-regulated structural systems, these materials allow humanity to construct buildings and orbital structures that appear physically impossible by real-world standards. Such architecture is not exempt from gravity or mechanical stress: many of its most spectacular forms remain continuously dependent upon powered stabilisation and can fail catastrophically if their supporting systems are interrupted; as seen in the devastating [[Collapse of Pandora (1964)]], in which the entire city suspended in the upper atmosphere of the [[Lilac]] gas giant suffered a systematic failure of all buoyancy-control systems due to a virus uploaded to its orbital control station in synchronous orbit around [[Lilac]] as part of a terrorist attack by the [[HPPO]].

Computers, machines that can be programmed to automatically carry out sequences of arithmetic or logical operations, are widespread throughout the [[Immediate Stellar Cluster]].

## Aesthetics
Visually, they are akin to real-world computers of the 1980s and still use [[Cathode Ray Tube|CRT]] displays, as well as  [[Magnetic Tape]] for data storage.

Computers are often built-in to dashboards to hide-away cables needed for power and networking, and to improve the devices' durability against physical damage and radiation damage when ex-atmo.

Furthermore, these dashboards are often built with modularity and maintainability in mind, allowing computers to be removed by a series of common-place screws and handles.

Many computers use P3 phosphor in their displays, so often many terminals are amber in hue.

## Usage & Deployment
Computers are a vital instrument on-board [[Spacecraft]], responsible for [[Isolation]] capabilities, [[Life Support]] systems, and basically every system a spacecraft would need to operate according to [[ITA]] convention.

Typically, a [[Spacecraft]] will have one centralised mainframe room, deep inside the hull and with specialised radiation protection, that terminals around the ship would connect to. This allowed the terminals themselves to be smaller (as less computational capacity was required), and cheaper, which was beneficial considering their weaker protection against cosmic radiation.  Individual terminals were capable of basic tasks themselves, such as external communications, simple arithmetic or logical operations, word processing, and timekeeping, however more computationally-demanding operations such as orbital calculations, trajectory planning, or the management of [[Isolation]] capabilities was left to the far more capable mainframe with each terminal sharing resources through a timeshare batch system.

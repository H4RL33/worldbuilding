The [[ISC Census]] is a comprehensive census legally mandated for all residents under [[ISCIA]] governance to complete every four years.

It aims to capture most of what a typical real-world census captures, as well as extra information that is specific to life in the [[Immediate Stellar Cluster]].
## Census Concluding Statement of Facts
The [[ISC Census#Census Concluding Statement of Facts|Census Concluding Statement of Facts]] is the ultimate product of the [[ISC Census]] and is produced through the [[ICRCM]] typically 3 - 4 months after the census has been adequately responded to.

The structure and goal of this document is to provide factual references useful to any committee under the [[ISCIA]], concisely and objectively without suggesting conclusions.

## Annual Census
There exists a smaller version of the [[ISC Census]] that runs annually and is not legally mandated. It's goal is to simiarly produce a statement of facts, but moreso as an update to the one compiled from the [[ISC Census]] proper, to avoid stagnating and out-of-date information.

While it is not legally required for citizens to complete this census, several incentives do exist:
- [[ISCIA]] contribution-matching to one's state pension, 30% over the next 6 months.
- 5 points to one's [[ISC Score]], which can bring with it many enticing benefits to one's quality of life.
- Other commendations such as medals and trophies.

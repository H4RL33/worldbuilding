[[Pandora]] was a luxury resort city suspended in the upper-atmosphere of [[Lilac]]. It comprised of 6 individual platforms, one large central hub, and a luxury spaceport with valet services; all linked with elegant [[MagLev]] transport lanes with trains that included on-board bars, viewing decks, and spacious luxury seating.

Everything on [[Pandora]] was included in an annual membership fee to the city; in which patrons would receive an identification card to show at venues for admission. Furthermore, each patron was entitled to a complimentary apartment during their stay.

There was no hard limit on how long a single patron may stay for nor how much of the included amenities they were allowed; however during the city's time there were numerous scandals involving high-profile celebrities overstaying their welcome with unfair usage of their membership.

## Economic Output
Despite being primarily a place for elite-class individuals to spend their money, [[Pandora]] had an industrial output of methane fuel harvested by each of the city's platforms and processed in a large industrial level of the city's central platform.

The [[Pandoran Council]] took exhaustive measures to ensure the city's industrial sector remained totally segregated from the experience for the elite patrons. Going so far as to contract the manufacturers of their patrons' usual spacecraft to create specialised models with vastly increased cargo capabilities while remaining visually discernible from the outside, and to disguise shipping crews in the usual attire of patrons.

At the peak of the city's economy, it's methane harvesting accounted for 43% of it's gross economic output.

## Collapse
[[Pandora]] was the victim of a high-profile terrorist attack by the [[HPPO]], which is further described in the [[Collapse of Pandora (1964)]].

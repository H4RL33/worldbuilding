A __Privateer__ is an adjacent-class citizen of the [[ISCIA]].

They are typically existing citizens of the [[ISCIA]] that voluntarily enlisted to become a privateer.

Privateers enjoy many more freedoms that typical citizens are not granted under the [[ISCIA]], here are a few examples:

- The right to own and operate private spacecraft.
- The right to lay claim and build-on open planetary territory.
- The right to the means of weapons, both hand-held and machined.
- The right to own and wear armour, for both combat and environmental protection.
- The right to regeneration and memory archiving.
- The right to free travel across the [[ISCIA]], star systems, planets, moons, [[Starports]], and planet-side settlements alike.

However, becoming a Privateer means the deliberate foregoing of several securities and safety-nets that [[ISCIA]] provides typical citizens, such as:

- The total ineligibility of any benefit from the welfare state.
- No guarantee of work.

Social prejudices against Privateers typically result in them having a harder time finding work, education, and habitation. Thankfully, the [[ISCIA]] is working with both Privateers, the private sector, and public re-education campaigns to reduce this prejudice, and has recently introduced a system to signify how reliable and secure any individual Privateer is, referred to as the [[Privateer Index]].

Having a higher grade on the [[Privateer Index]] is a very beneficial thing for Privateers looking for high-paying contracts, long-term work, insurance, and private security, as it typically increases chances of acceptance.

A Privateer's [[Privateer Index]] grade is increased by measuring their individual economic output, so completing contracted work, mining and refining raw material, discovering new celestial bodies, bounty hunting, and a myriad of other potential careers all contribute to a Privateer's grade. Furthermore, a Privateer's grade improvement can be boosted by completing contracted work well before the deadline, or by staying consistent with the same employer, as this shows reliability and dedication.

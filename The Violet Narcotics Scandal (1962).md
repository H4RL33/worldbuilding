[[The Violet Narcotics Scandal (1962)]] was a scandal involving the luxury city [[Pandora]] and several key members of an underground, covert narcotics production ring in [[Pandora]]'s industrial levels.

The ring, comprised of key names such as [[Lisa Sims]], [[Robert Brown]], [[Michael Taylor]], and [[Susan Moore]], as well as an expected few-dozen other industrial workers, manufactured an authentic narcotic known by the street-name [[Violet]] by capturing xenon gas from the [[Lilac]]an atmosphere and manufacturing a pseudo-opioid from it.

[[Violet]] was sold largely to patrons of [[Pandora]] in bars, casinos, and other luxury installations by "top-siders" (the colloquial name of [[Pandora]]n workers and permanent inhabitants of the luxury part of the city) who were in on the ring and received a share of the product they helped move.

It is believed that the [[Violet]] ring operated from 1956 - 1962, until two top-siders were arrested after mistakenly offering [[Violet]] to a local authority without knowing. The arrests led to an increased security presence in venues across [[Pandora]] and the industrial levels, leading to the ring to be disbanded as it was no longer viable to move meaningful amounts of product.

Furthermore, [[Violet]] has been found off-world outside [[Lilac]], and so it is unclear as to whether buyers of the product may have taken some supply with them after their departure, or if the ring managed to get product through the industrial spaceport of [[Pandora]]; no suspicious shipping manifests have been found however.

The ring is estimated to have made upwards of 300,000,000 [[SLO]].

[[Violet]] is a xenon-derived narcotic with effects similar to opioids.
## Emergence
[[Violet]] originated in the lost city of [[Pandora]] during the [[The Violet Narcotics Scandal (1962)]].

By a co-ordinated multi-level ring throughout [[Pandora]], [[Violet]] was able to be manufactured, moved, and sold to various buyers on [[Pandora]].
## Post-Scandal Presence
The recipe of [[Violet]] was shared on various illicit forums, by official ring-leader [[Lisa Sims]] after she knew the ring would dissolve.

Because of this, [[Violet]] exists in many different forms in many different parts of the [[Local Cluster]], and can be found rather easily.
## Cultural Significance
- During [[Annual Pandoran Reflection Day]], some take [[Violet]] as a symbolic gesture of experiencing the spirit of the city.
- [[Violet]] is often touted as the only lasting legacy of [[Pandora]].
